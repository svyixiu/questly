How it works

Three steps.
No game needed.

Discord knows you're playing by spotting a game's program running on your PC. Questly starts a tiny stand-in with the same file name, so Discord spots it the same way.

  1. 1

    Find the game

    Press Ctrl+K and search Discord's list of over 20,000 detectable games for the one your quest asks for.

  2. 2

    Press Play

    Questly starts the stand-in program. It doesn't need the real game, doesn't use your Discord login, and doesn't change anything inside Discord.

  3. 3

    Discord sees you playing

    Your quest starts counting. Stop whenever you like, or let a timer stop it for you.

Timed runs

Time that actually counts.

Discord can take a minute to notice a game. Questly waits for it: each countdown only starts once Discord reports the game, so a 15-minute timer means 15 minutes of quest progress. Run everything at once, or one after another in a fixed or random order, and it all stops by itself.

Features

Everything a quest needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Search every detectable game

Spotlight search over Discord's whole list, with fuzzy matching that stays smooth while you type. Games you already have are hidden.

One click, or all of them

Play one game, check a few, or Launch all. Every action has a keyboard shortcut; press ? in the app to see them.

Game windows your way

Each game gets a small square window in Questly's style that you can drag anywhere. Keep it on screen, park it off-screen or hide it. It never steals focus.

Performance Guard

During timed runs it watches CPU, memory and stutter. If your PC struggles, it closes a few games for a while and brings them back with their time left.

Panic Abort

One button or Ctrl+Shift+X stops every game, the timer and anything running in the background, then tells you exactly what it stopped.

Legitimate Buddy

While you're away, it opens a few games from your library like you would, within the hours, days and limits you set, and stops the moment you're back.

Make it yours

Six themes, any accent color, or a fully custom theme with a color for every part. Even the taskbar icon follows your accent.

Your library is a file

Your games live in a plain library.json you can edit by hand, export, or swap with friends. Import merges with yours or replaces it.

One file that installs itself

The same Questly.exe installs, launches and uninstalls. Everything is per-user, so no admin rights, and removing it leaves nothing behind.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is it free?
Yes. Questly is free and open source under the MIT License. There are no accounts, ads or paid features.
Can it get my Discord account banned?
It can. Pretending to play a game breaks Discord's Terms of Service, and Discord may remove rewards, warn, suspend or ban accounts that do it. Questly can't prevent that, and makes no promise that it won't happen. Only use it if you accept that risk.
Does Questly need my Discord login or token?
No. It never asks for your account and never reads Discord's saved sign-in data. To know when Discord has picked up a game, it reads one kind of line from Discord's own log file on your PC, and nothing from that file leaves your computer.
Which quests does it work for?
Quests that ask you to play a desktop game for some time. Quests that require streaming to friends, playing on a console, or doing something inside the game aren't covered, and Discord can change how quests work at any time.
Why does Windows warn me when I open it?
Questly isn't code-signed yet, and Windows SmartScreen warns about unsigned apps it hasn't seen much. Click More info → Run anyway. You can compare the file's SHA-256 with the one on this page, or build Questly yourself from the source.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
Not yet. Questly is built for Windows 10 and 11. The live demo works in any modern browser.
Where is my data kept?
Only on your PC: your games in %APPDATA%\Questly\library.json, the stand-in programs in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Questly, and your settings inside the app. See the Privacy Policy for everything it reads and connects to.
How do I uninstall it?
In Questly, go to Settings → Installation → Uninstall, or use Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Questly. You can choose to remove your library and settings too.
